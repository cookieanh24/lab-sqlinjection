SQL Injection Lab

Lab to chuc theo phan loai chuan cua SQL injection. Xem README.md de biet cach khai thac thu cong chi tiet.

A. In-band SQLi (du lieu ve tren cung kenh)

  • Error-based → /products?id=1
  • UNION-based → /products?id=1

B. Inferential / Blind SQLi (suy luan, khong lo du lieu)

  • Boolean-based → /check?username=admin
  • Time-based → /track

C. Out-of-band (OOB) SQLi (rut du lieu qua kenh khac)

  • OOB → /lookup (can chay oob_collector.py, xem collector /log)

D. Vector bo sung (minh hoa impact)

  • Authentication bypass → /login
  • Stacked queries → /feedback
  • Second-order → /register rồi /profile

Muc tieu CTF: trich xuat flag trong bang admin_secrets va mat khau cua admin bang tung ky thuat.
